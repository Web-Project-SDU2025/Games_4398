Audio sample source
===================

ball.wav    <- "球与球碰.wav"   (ball-to-ball contact)
pocket.wav  <- "球进袋.wav"    (ball entering a pocket)

Both files are byte-for-byte copies of audio supplied with the Shandong
University Object-Oriented Technology course example project. The lecturer
approved their use in this project.

The source recordings are kept unchanged. At runtime, SampleBank converts the
stereo files to mono, removes leading silence, and normalizes their peak level.
AudioConfig contains the related playback settings.

Original format: 48000 Hz, 16-bit stereo. The two channels contain the same
mono recording. The leading silence is approximately 57 ms for ball.wav and
100 ms for pocket.wav.
